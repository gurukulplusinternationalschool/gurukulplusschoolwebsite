About Us

A Legacy of Holistic Education & Innovation

Gurukul Plus International School & Junior College is committed to providing quality education that nurtures every child's potential. Our 2-acre campus provides a safe, modern, and inspiring environment.

We blend traditional values with modern pedagogy, ensuring students are prepared for the challenges of tomorrow while staying rooted in culture and ethics.
